A deadpan one-star review of sleep — an everyday essential everyone relies on — written for the [make.fun bounty "One-Star Review of Something Essential"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-0xb3e689-one-star-review-of-something-essential-c7234629).

## The entry

> Sleep: I give it eight hours and it gives me back a sore neck and the feeling I forgot something. The worst trade I make every single day.

One line, 138 characters, inside the 160-character gate. The grievance is the small daily annoyance everyone recognises but has never seen written down: you hand sleep eight hours and wake up with a sore neck and the nagging feeling you forgot something. The sting lands in the last few words, deadpan throughout — no winking, no exclamation marks, one joke told cleanly.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13 standard library only, deterministic, no network) writes the review to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` for local runs) and self-checks the competition gate before and after writing: UTF-8, 1–160 characters, a single line, not only whitespace.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
cat out/entry.txt
```
